FloodTect Device Components

  • • Housing: Smoke detector-style ceiling mount enclosure
  • • Sensor: JSN-SR04T waterproof ultrasonic distance sensor (enclosed inside)
  • • LED Indicator: Green = Normal, Red/Flashing = Flood Alert
  • • Microcontroller: Arduino/ESP32 for processing and alerts
  • • Power: Hardwired or battery backup

How It Works

  1. 1. Normal Operation: FloodTect measures 8 feet (ceiling to floor)
  2. 2. Water Detection: As water rises, distance decreases
  3. 3. Alert Trigger: When distance drops below threshold (e.g., 7 feet = 1 foot of water)
  4. 4. Notification: LED flashes red, buzzer sounds, WiFi alert sent

  • • Mount FloodTect devices flat on ceiling (like smoke detectors) with sensor facing straight down
  • • Standard ceiling height: 8 feet - calibrate for your specific measurements
  • • Set alert threshold: Recommend trigger at 7 feet (= 1 foot of water) for early warning
  • • Multiple zones: Install one FloodTect device per room/area to monitor
  • • WiFi connectivity: Add for remote monitoring and smartphone alerts
  • • Power backup: Include battery backup in case of power outage during flooding
